# Customer Churn & Retention Analytics

An end-to-end customer churn analytics project that combines exploratory data analysis, machine learning, business-oriented threshold optimization, model explainability, customer risk segmentation, and retention strategy recommendations.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn directly affects recurring revenue and customer lifetime value.

This project analyzes customer churn for a telecommunications provider using the **IBM Telco Customer Churn dataset** and develops a predictive framework to identify customers at higher risk of leaving.

Rather than focusing only on model accuracy, the project connects predictive modeling with a practical business objective:

> **Identify customers at risk of churn and prioritize retention efforts where they are most needed.**

The project follows the complete analytical workflow:

```text
Data Cleaning
      ↓
Exploratory Data Analysis
      ↓
